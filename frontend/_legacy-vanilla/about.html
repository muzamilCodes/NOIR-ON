Since 1974

A maison built on patience

NOIRÉON began in a single-room atelier with one watchmaker, one lathe, and an obsession with the way light moves across a polished case. Fifty years later, that obsession hasn't changed — only the number of hands that share it.

We still assemble every movement by hand. We still fire our enamel dials seven times. And we still produce fewer than 900 pieces a year, because a timepiece that outlives its owner cannot be rushed.
